Vaults are separate now.

A second repository used to appear as a folder inside the first, which meant search mixed them and a link in one could resolve into the other. Each is now its own vault: its own files, index, search and tasks, and links that stay where they were written. The browser switches between them.

Files already on the device are moved rather than fetched again, so nothing re-downloads. The index is rebuilt from what is on disk, which takes a few seconds.
