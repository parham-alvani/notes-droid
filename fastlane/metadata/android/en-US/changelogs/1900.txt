Notes open in tabs. A vault is read by holding two or three notes at once — the one being read, the one it links to, and the index they both hang off — and a back stack makes that a line you can only walk backwards.

Tapping a link now shows where it goes before it goes there: the title, where it lives, and its opening words, with the choice of opening it here or in a new tab. A link that resolves to nothing says so instead of doing nothing.

Back closes the tab you are reading, and leaves the reader when it was the last one.

Widgets fit their rows more carefully; the last one was being drawn off the bottom edge.
