The tabs are still there when you come back. Leaving the app and returning restores every open note, the trail each tab has been through, and which one you were reading.

They are remembered by path rather than by note number, so they survive a reindex — a note number is a row that gets handed out again, and reindexing happens whenever the app learns to extract something new. A note that has since been renamed or removed drops out instead of opening nothing.
