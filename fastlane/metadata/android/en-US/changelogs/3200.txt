Made for reading.

Fold a section shut from its heading: every heading with something under it has a chevron at the end of the line, as in Obsidian's reading view. The outline, a link to a heading, find in note and read aloud all open what they need on the way there.

The bar at the top slides away as you read down and comes back on the first scroll up, and a hairline under it shows how far through the note you are.

A new switch in Settings, Behaviour, keeps the screen on while a note is open, for a recipe or a checklist followed rather than read.
