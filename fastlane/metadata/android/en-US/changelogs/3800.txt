A smaller, more colourful app.

Text is a step smaller throughout, so more of a note fits on the screen; the text size setting still makes it larger. Folders, notes and attachments in a list sit on a tile in their own colour -- or the one Iconic gives them -- and the selected tab, chips and buttons are tinted by their colour instead of grey. Corners are rounder.

In the light theme, headings, italics, code and links are now drawn in colours made for paper. Headings used to be pale yellow on white and hard to read.
