Attachments open now.

Tapping a PDF, video, audio file, archive or office document in a note hands it to whatever app on the phone opens that kind of file. The card has always been there and tapping it did nothing; nothing was wired behind it, and the files were not synced either.

They are recorded during sync and fetched the first time one is asked for, the same way images already worked, so a default install still carries only the markdown.
