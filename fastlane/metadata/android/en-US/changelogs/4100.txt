A new Calendar tab shows the next two weeks: the meetings from the calendars on the phone, and the tasks due or scheduled on each day. Tap a meeting to open it in the calendar app, or a task to open its note.

In Settings > Calendar, choose the calendars to show, mark a calendar "Busy only", and choose a vault for week notes. Each sync then writes one note per week, such as Calendar/2026-W43.md, from last week to eight weeks out.
