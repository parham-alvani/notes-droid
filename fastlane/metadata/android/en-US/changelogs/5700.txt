A task can say why it waits or why it is cancelled, in a [reason:: …] field that Obsidian's Tasks plugin keeps. The board, the task lists and the reader show the reason, and cancelling asks for one. Task statuses now come from the vault's Tasks plugin settings, so a custom status such as [r] In Review goes in the right column with its name. Queries take has reason, reason includes and status.name. Home screen widgets follow a new background or theme at once.
