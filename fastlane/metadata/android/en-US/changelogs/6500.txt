A vault can be read-only on this phone: Settings, Vaults has a Read-only switch. Daftar then only pulls, never commits or pushes, and refuses every change with a notice. The same card shows the address the vault pulls from and lets you change it.
A vault can carry the app's settings in its .daftar.toml (theme, text size, sync, the home picture and more). A setting from the vault says so, and Settings can decline all of them or one.
A note with a type in its front matter is a concept of the Open Knowledge Format: its properties show how far it is verified, a deprecated or stale concept says so above its text, and Mark as Verified records your review.
Markdown links to notes now count as links, in backlinks and the graph. Each vault is indexed again once after this update.
