Unlinked mentions, a home screen widget, and sharing.

The backlinks sheet now has a second section: notes that say this one's name without linking to it. In a vault where every link is typed by hand, that is where most of the connections somebody meant to make actually are.

A widget shows what is overdue and due today without opening the app, and refreshes after each sync. Tapping it opens the task list.

A note can be shared as markdown rather than as rendered text, so what arrives is something the other end can use.
